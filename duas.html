PRAYERS FOR YOUR NEW CHAPTER

May Allah
bless you.

Some wishes are spoken. Some are whispered directly to Allah.

01

Peace

May Allah fill your heart with a peace that no situation can take away.

02

Protection

May Allah protect you from every visible and invisible harm, and surround you with sincere people.

03

Rizq

May Allah grant you abundant, halal and blessed rizq, and put barakah in everything you receive.

04

Success

May every sincere effort you make become a doorway to something better than you imagined.

05

Happiness

May Allah give you reasons to smile on ordinary days and beautiful memories to treasure forever.

06

Dreams

May the dreams you carry quietly in your heart become realities at the perfect time.

07

Guidance

May Allah guide every decision you make and lead you toward people and paths that are good for you.

08

Future

May your future be softer, brighter and more beautiful than anything you could have planned yourself.

“

May Allah write your name among those who are loved, protected, guided and blessed.

Ameen.
