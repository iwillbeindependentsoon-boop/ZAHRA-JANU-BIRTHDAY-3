For Someone Truly Special

✦
Dear Zahra, With duas & love ♡

Today is more than just another date on the calendar. It is a reminder that Allah chose this day to bring you into this world — and that alone is something worth celebrating with a full heart.

On your birthday, I pray that Allah surrounds you with people who are sincere, hearts that are gentle with yours, and moments that make you quietly grateful to be alive. May He protect your heart from every sadness you never speak about and replace every difficult chapter with something more beautiful than you imagined.

May Allah put barakah in your age, your health, your dreams, your studies, your work, your relationships, your home, and every step you take toward your future. May He open doors for you that you never knew existed and close every door that would bring you harm.

I pray that the wishes you keep quietly inside your heart are answered at exactly the right time. May your duas come back to you as beautiful realities, and may you always have the sabr to wait for what is written for you and the tawakkul to trust Allah's timing.

May you never have to beg for the love, respect, loyalty, or kindness you deserve. May the people around you celebrate your happiness, protect your peace, and remind you of your worth whenever you forget it.

And whenever life feels heavy, may Allah make your heart strong without making it hard. May He give you courage without taking away your softness, confidence without taking away your humility, and success without taking away your peace.

I hope this year gives you more reasons to smile, more memories to keep, more places to see, more dreams to chase, more laughter that hurts your cheeks, and more evenings where you look back and think, “Alhamdulillah, I made it here.”

Please keep being the beautiful person you are. Keep your heart kind. Keep your standards high. Keep believing that good things can find you. And most importantly, keep making dua — because Allah can turn the smallest hope into a life you never imagined.

May Allah protect you, bless you, guide you, forgive you, increase you in goodness, grant you halal happiness, give you a long life filled with khair, and make every coming year of your life better than the one before it.

Happy Birthday, Zahra. 🤍
May your life always be written with beautiful chapters.
