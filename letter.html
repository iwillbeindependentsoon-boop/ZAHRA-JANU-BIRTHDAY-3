A FEW WORDS FOR YOU

Dear
Zahra,

There are some people who enter our lives quietly, but somehow leave their presence everywhere.

You are one of those people.

On your birthday, I don't just want to say “Happy Birthday.” I want to remind you that you are worthy of beautiful things, sincere people, peaceful days and dreams that actually come true.

I hope you never become so busy growing that you forget how far you've already come.

I hope you continue becoming the woman you've always imagined yourself to be — confident, graceful, kind and impossible to replace.

And whenever life becomes difficult, I hope you remember that difficult chapters are still chapters. They don't define the whole story.

May Allah protect your heart from what isn't meant for you, bring you closer to what is good for you, and open doors you never even knew existed.

May your future hold more happiness than your past ever did.

With love,
Someone who wishes you the best. 🤍
