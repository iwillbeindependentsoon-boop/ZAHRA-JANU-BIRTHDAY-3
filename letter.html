01 A MOMENT WORTH KEEPING
A FEW WORDS FOR YOU

Dear
Zahra.

Some people enter our lives quietly, but somehow leave their presence everywhere.

You are one of those people.

Today, I don't just want to say “Happy Birthday.”

I want to remind you that you deserve beautiful things, sincere people, peaceful days and dreams that genuinely come true.

I hope you never become so busy growing that you forget how far you've already come.

May this new chapter bring you closer to everything your heart quietly prays for.
